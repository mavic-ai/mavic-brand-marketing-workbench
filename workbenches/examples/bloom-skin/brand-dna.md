# Bloom Skin: Brand DNA (fictional demo brand)

Bloom Skin is invented for teaching and for screen recordings. It is not a real company.

## Brand basics
- One line: gentle, barrier-first skincare for sensitive skin

## Personality
- Calm, not clinical
- Honest about ingredients
- Warm and practical
- Quietly confident

## Writing style: LinkedIn
- Tone: warm, plain and expert, without jargon
- Structure: a one-line hook, two or three short paragraphs, one practical tip, then a soft close
- Opens with something noticed from customers or in the lab. Never opens with a question.
- Closes with a practical next step. No hard sell.
- Sentences mostly under 15 words. No emoji. Two hashtags at most.

## Ban list
- Start from a suggested list of AI cliches, then add: holy grail, flawless

## Visual identity
- Logo: plain wordmark, "Bloom Skin", set in Fraunces in Clay
- Colours: Petal #F4C7C3, Clay #B5654A, Ink #2B2B2B, Cream #FBF6F0
- Fonts: Fraunces for headings, Inter for body
- Rules: plenty of white space; products shown on Cream; no more than two colours plus Cream in any layout

## Customer profile: Sensitive-skin Sam
- Age 25 to 40, with skin that reacts easily
- Reads ingredient lists before buying
- Wary of hype; buys on trust and on friends' recommendations

## Products and services
- Barrier Serum: a lightweight serum with ceramides that supports the skin barrier. No medical or results claims.
