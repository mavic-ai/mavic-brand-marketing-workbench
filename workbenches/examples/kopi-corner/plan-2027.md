## Ideas for the moments we join

### Plastic Free July

- **Core idea:** bring your own cup and your kopi is 20 cents off, all July.
- **Why our customer cares:** most of our regulars take away, and they'd rather not bin a cup every morning.
- **Proof:** the cup count, posted every Friday.
- **What we'll do (not just post):** the discount, and a shelf of donated jars for anyone who forgets.
- **Instagram:** Photo: the first jar of the month on the counter. "1 July, 6.42am. First bring-your-own cup of the month. 20 cents off your kopi, all July. What are you bringing? ♻️"
- **Facebook:** "All of July, bring your own cup and your kopi is 20 cents off at stall 15. We'll post how many cups you saved every Friday."
- **Watch out:** don't call the shop plastic-free. It isn't.

### International Day of Older Persons

- **Core idea:** Mdm Lee (fictional) has started her day with us for 22 years.
- **Why our customer cares:** our regulars know her, and see themselves in her.
- **Proof:** her own words, collected before we post, and her permission.
- **What we'll do (not just post):** a large-print menu at the counter from 1 Oct.
- **Instagram:** Photo: Mdm Lee at her usual table. "Mdm Lee has had her kopi-O here for 22 years. This week we're asking her what's changed. What should we ask her? ☕"
- **Facebook:** "From 1 October, there's a large-print menu at our counter. Mdm Lee, here every morning for 22 years, helped us choose the size. Ask for it next time you visit."
- **Watch out:** no stereotypes. She's a regular, not a symbol.

### World Food Day

- **Core idea:** yesterday's bread becomes today's kaya bread pudding.
- **Why our customer cares:** nobody likes seeing good bread thrown away.
- **Proof:** loaves saved since September: [number, from our count].
- **What we'll do (not just post):** the pudding, every day from 16 Oct.
- **Instagram:** "Yesterday's bread. Today's kaya bread pudding. [number] loaves saved since September. Tried it yet? 🍞"
- **Facebook:** "From 16 October, our day-old bread becomes kaya bread pudding, $1.50 a slice. We've saved [number] loaves from the bin since September. Ask for a slice with your kopi."
- **Watch out:** only post the number once we've counted it.

### World Kindness Day

- **Core idea:** a kopi on the board is a kopi someone has paid forward.
- **Why our customer cares:** it's a small thing they can do on the way to work.
- **Proof:** the board, and the count of kopi given.
- **What we'll do (not just post):** we match the first 50 kopi paid forward, from 13 Nov.
- **Instagram:** Photo: the board with the first note on it. "One kopi on the board. Someone paid it forward this morning. We'll match the first 50. Who would you buy one for? ☕"
- **Facebook:** "From 13 November, you can pay for a kopi for someone who needs one, and we'll match the first 50. Ask at the counter."
- **Watch out:** it's about giving, not a reason to buy. No "buy one to be kind".
