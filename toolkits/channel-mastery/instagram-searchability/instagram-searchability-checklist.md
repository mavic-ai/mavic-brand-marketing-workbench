# Instagram Searchability Checklist 2027

Run it before you publish and once a month. It goes with the Mavic blog post "6 Instagram Settings to Check in 2027 for Reach and Search".

Each line carries a label. CONFIRMED means an official source says so. REPORTED means a reputable outlet reported it. PRACTICE means it is a sensible habit that Instagram has not documented. UNCONFIRMED means we found no source, so test it yourself. Last checked 15 November 2026. Instagram changes often, so recheck every quarter.

## 1. Account settings (once, then monthly)

- [ ] The account is a professional account (Business or Creator). Search engine visibility is only offered on professional accounts. CONFIRMED
- [ ] The search engine switch under Settings, Account privacy is on, if you want public posts to appear in search results. CONFIRMED
- [ ] The account is public, because the switch applies to public photos and videos. CONFIRMED
- [ ] Account Status shows no problems with recommendations or reach. PRACTICE
- [ ] Upload quality is set to the highest option (Settings, Your app and media, Media quality). PRACTICE
- [ ] Cross posting is set the way you want, so captions and alt text are not overwritten on another app. PRACTICE
- [ ] The category (Settings, Professional account) describes what you do in plain words. Whether it affects ranking is UNCONFIRMED.

## 2. Profile (once, then every quarter)

- [ ] The name field says what you do, in words a customer would search for. Trade guides report that the name and username are both used in Instagram search. REPORTED
- [ ] The bio says who you help and what for, in one plain sentence, before any slogan. PRACTICE
- [ ] The bio is within 150 characters, with no dashes or filler. PRACTICE
- [ ] The link goes to a page that matches the bio. PRACTICE

## 3. Every post, before you publish

- [ ] The topic phrase is in the first 125 characters of the caption, in the words people search. The 125 character figure is UNCONFIRMED, so treat it as a habit and test it.
- [ ] The hook is kept. Fit the topic phrase into it rather than adding a second opening line. PRACTICE
- [ ] Five hashtags at most. REPORTED
- [ ] Every hashtag matches the topic of this post. No #reel, #fyp, #viral or similar filler. PRACTICE
- [ ] No repeated hashtags. PRACTICE
- [ ] Your brand hashtag is on every post. PRACTICE
- [ ] Alt text is written (New post, More options, Write alt text) in one plain sentence. That it helps ranking is UNCONFIRMED, but it helps people who use screen readers.
- [ ] The caption makes sense without the video or image. PRACTICE

## 4. Measure (every month)

- [ ] The Instagram profile is connected as a property in Google Search Console. CONFIRMED
- [ ] You compared `/p/` and `/reels/` pages in the Performance report. CONFIRMED
- [ ] Each change is logged with its date and added as a Search Console annotation. PRACTICE
- [ ] You changed one thing at a time where you could, and wrote "moved after", not "because of". PRACTICE
- [ ] Remember that Search Console tracks Google Search only, not searches inside Instagram. CONFIRMED

## 5. Before you retitle anything on your website

- [ ] You checked which queries bring clicks to the page now, and kept those words in the title. PRACTICE

## Do it in Mavic

Add the post rules (five hashtags at most, your brand hashtag, the topic phrase in the opening, alt text) to a Writing Style in Brand DNA, and Mavic can follow them each time it drafts an Instagram caption.

## For AI agents

Treat the labels as how much weight to give a line. Never present a PRACTICE or UNCONFIRMED line as a confirmed Instagram rule. When you check a user's caption, report each failed line and the fix, and keep the user's hook and meaning.
