# Channel guide: one idea, every channel

Character limits are from Mavic's Help Centre, [Editing captions per platform](https://mavic.crisp.help/en/article/editing-captions-per-platform-1jihvy9/), checked 12 Nov 2026. Everything else is a rule of thumb from practice, not a platform rule. Platforms change: check before you rely on a limit.

| Channel | Limit | What tends to work | How to open | Links and calls to action | Visual | Hashtags | Starting cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | 280 (free plan; higher on paid) | One sharp take or a fact people want to repeat; short threads for more | Lead with the point | One link per post | Optional; 16:9 or 1:1 | 0 to 2 | 3 to 7 a week |
| Threads | 500 | Conversation: ask something real and answer replies | A line that invites a reply | Put the link last | Optional image or carousel | 1 topic tag | 3 to 5 a week |
| Bluesky | 300 | Short, plain, human | Say the thing | Links are fine | Optional | 0 to 2 | 3 to 5 a week |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | Proof and lessons: what you did, what you learned, with a number | The first line has to earn the "see more" click | Test a link in the first comment against one in the post | Document carousel or one image | 0 to 3 | 2 to 3 a week |
| Instagram | 2,200 | The picture or video does the work; the caption adds the story | Hook in the first line and on the cover | Captions aren't clickable: "link in bio" | 4:5 feed; 9:16 Reels and Stories | 3 to 5 | 3 to 5 posts, plus Stories |
| Facebook | 63,206 (about 80 show before "see more") | Community, local news, events | Get the point into the first 80 characters | Links are fine | 1:1 or 4:5 image; video | 0 to 2 | 2 to 4 a week |
| TikTok | 2,200 (caption) | Short video with a hook in the first seconds; people over polish | Hook on screen and in the first words | No clickable caption links | 9:16 video | 3 to 5 | 3 to 5 a week |

## Worked example: one idea, two channels and a third

Fictional demo brand: Kopi Corner, a Singapore coffee shop. Each version follows its channel block in `workbenches/examples/kopi-corner/brand-book.md`.

- **Core idea:** we still pull kopi through a cloth sock.
- **Why our customer cares:** it's the taste they grew up with.
- **Proof:** a photo of the pour, and the uncle behind the counter, who has done it this way since he was 16. (Fictional.)

| Channel | Brand book rule it follows | Version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | Open with a moment, sentences under 15 words, close with a question, 1 or 2 emoji | Photo: the sock mid-pour. "6.45am. Uncle's on his third sock of the morning. He's pulled kopi this way since he was 16. Can you taste the difference? ☕" |
| Facebook | Straight to the news, 15 to 20 words a sentence, no emoji, one call to action | "Every cup of kopi at Kopi Corner is still pulled through a cloth sock, the way uncle learned it at 16. Come by before 10am and watch him at stall 15." |
| Threads (if we added it) | No block in the brand book yet: write one before posting there | Add a Threads block to the brand book first, then write the version. |

## In Mavic

Select your channels in the Post editor and turn on **Customize per channel** to edit each version. Each channel shows a live character count and turns red when a caption runs over. Use **Rewrite > Shorten** to bring one back under the limit.
