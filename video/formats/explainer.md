# Format: explainer

One marketing concept, told from its history to the AI era, ending on the first principle. Paired with a how-to on the same topic; the two are uploaded together and link to each other.

## Shape (4 to 6 minutes)

| Beat | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | A concrete puzzle in one image (two near-identical products, one costs twice as much) |
| Scroll back | The page flicks back through time to the oldest era |
| Eras | One scene per era. Each era shows the idea doing a new job. Years appear as designed objects in the scene (a carved slab, a shop sign, a road sign, a calendar page), never as captions |
| Now | What AI changes, and what it makes harder |
| First principle | Rewind through every era, land on a clean page, state the principle in one sentence |
| Why follow | What the channel does and what the brand is building. Spoken only |
| Sources card | The only dense text: every source, with full links in the description |
| Logo close | Silent |

## Visual rules

- Paper collage in light mode: cut-paper objects with a white sticker edge, soft drop shadow, slight stop-motion jitter.
- One continuity object (the seal) travels through every scene, so each transition has something moving from one scene into the next.
- Moving between eras is a vertical scroll, with motion blur and a time ruler on the right edge.
- No captions or labels over the picture. Supers (short paper-strip titles) are allowed at a handful of key moments, for example "The story of a brand".

## Sound

- One synthetic voice for every explainer (Mavic uses Google Chirp 3 HD Achird), one file per line, mixed to -16 LUFS.
- Paper sound effects tied to the animation: pencil scribble on every hand-drawn line, whoosh and page flutter on every scroll through time, a soft thump when the continuity object lands. Ducked under the voice.
- Pauses longer than about a second can be played faster in the edit (Mavic uses 2.5x), never during speech.

## Script pack

Brief (purpose, who for, playlist, search phrases, runtime), titles, description with chapters and sources, full script table (picture | voice), sources card text, voiceover table (id | at (s) | line), Shorts, claims check with a source per claim, open questions.
