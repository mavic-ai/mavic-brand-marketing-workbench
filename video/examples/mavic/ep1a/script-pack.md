# Episode 1A: Explainer: What is a brand?

A real script pack from the Mavic YouTube channel, shared as a worked example of the explainer format (`video/formats/explainer.md`). Version 2, 10 Nov 2026: paper-collage style, light mode, no text over the picture apart from a few supers. Pair 1 with Ep 1B; the two are uploaded together and 1A links to 1B. Source for the film: `video/remotion/paper-explainer/`.

## Brief

- **Purpose:** The viewer understands why brands exist, from the first marks of ownership to brands that AI recommends, and leaves with the first principle: a brand is a promise, made distinctive, kept consistently.
- **Who it is for:** Founders, new marketers and small business owners who use the word brand every day but have never been shown what it is for.
- **Playlist:** Explainers
- **Search phrases:** what is a brand; history of branding; why do brands exist; brand psychology
- **Runtime:** 4:41 in the v2 edit (pauses over about a second played at 2.5x, never during speech or the sources card), 5:08 as scripted at the brand voice's default rate (timings below are estimates at 150 words a minute; the creation agent re-times from the rendered audio)
- **Channel:** Mavic YouTube, faceless. 16:9 at 1920x1080, plus two 9:16 Shorts

## Edit v2 (11 Nov 2026)

Choosing is risky and 2. People want to belong; 263.2 A brand is a promise, made distinctive, kept consistently. (bottom centre). Times are on the scripted timeline.
- SFX: procedural paper sounds placed on the animation's own events (pencil scribbles on every drawn line and question mark, whooshes and page flutter on every scroll through time, a soft thump each time the seal lands, sizzle on the cattle brand, typewriter, card flicks, machine whir). Ducked under the voice.
- Pace: silences longer than about a second are played at 2.5x, keeping 0.35 s either side of speech. Saves 27 s. Done in the edit, not re-rendered.
