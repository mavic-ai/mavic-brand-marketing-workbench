# Curation rules: Master List of 2027 Social Media Holidays

A moment is on the list only if it passes **all four** tests.

| # | Test | What counts | What doesn't |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **A movement runs it** | A named organiser with an official site and a stated cause: a UN agency, a charity, a coalition | A brand, a calendar website, or a "national X day" generator |
| 2 | **People take part** | It has run for 3 years or more, and the public takes part beyond brand posts: events, pledges, fundraising, clean-ups, switch-offs | Days that exist only as hashtags for brands |
| 3 | **There's an ask** | It asks people to do something specific: give, switch off, pledge, clean up, check in, learn | "Celebrate" with nothing to do |
| 4 | **Our markets observe it** | It's observed in at least one of Singapore, the Philippines, Australia, New Zealand or the United States. Each entry lists its markets | Moments observed only outside those markets |

## Left out on purpose

- **Sensitive days:** World Suicide Prevention Day, R U OK? Day, World AIDS Day, International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, and remembrance and memorial days. These matter, but brand content around them can hurt people. Support quietly, if at all.
- **Brand-founded or commercial days:** Small Business Saturday (started by American Express), Black Friday, Cyber Monday, 11.11 (Singles' Day), Prime Day. Plan these as sales moments if you sell, but they aren't movements.
- **Novelty days:** National Donut Day, Talk Like a Pirate Day and the like. Fun, but there's no movement and no ask.
- **Public and religious holidays:** Lunar New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali, Christmas, Waitangi Day, Thanksgiving and the rest. Mavic's Calendar shows public holidays for each country in its Holidays panel; any calendar app does too.

## Judgement calls, and why

- **Mental health days** (World Mental Health Day, Mental Health Awareness Month): kept. They are widely run, with clear asks. Each entry says "support, don't sell" and to link official services.
- **Heritage and inclusion moments** (Black History Month, Pride Month, NAIDOC Week): kept, with strict "join only if" lines. They are movements, and joining them without a real relationship does harm.
- **Fashion Revolution Week:** kept. Its UK headquarters closed in 2026 and the movement now runs through its global network. The 2027 dates aren't announced yet; it is anchored on 24 April, the Rana Plaza anniversary.
- **Earth Overshoot Day:** kept, but its date is calculated each year and announced around June. In 2026 it fell on 30 July.

## Each entry has

Date or range in 2027, name, type, organiser and link, cause, the ask, hashtag, markets, natural fit, **join only if**, three content angles, when to start planning, **watch out**, source, and the date it was checked.

## Suggesting a moment

Open an issue with the organiser's official link and a line for each test above. We rebuild the list each November for the next year.
