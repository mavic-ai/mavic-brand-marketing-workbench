## Dates and organisers

- **UN observances** (fixed dates for every UN day on the list): United Nations, List of International Days and Weeks. https://www.un.org/en/observances/list-days-weeks
- **Safer Internet Day 2027:** Tuesday 9 February 2027; activities run 1 to 28 February. Better Internet for Kids (Insafe, for the European Commission). https://better-internet-for-kids.europa.eu/en/events/safer-internet-day-sid-2027
- **Earth Hour 2027:** Saturday 20 March 2027, 8:30 pm local time, per WWF's Earth Hour site (checked 15 Nov 2026). https://www.earthhour.org
- **Earth Day 2027 theme**, Food for Our Planet, launched by EARTHDAY.ORG at the World Food Forum, 14 October 2026. Reported by vegconomist, 20 October 2026. https://vegconomist.com/organisations-and-brands/earthday/
- **Fashion Revolution:** UK headquarters closed in 2026; the movement continues through its global network. Fashion Revolution Week 2026 ran 22 to 28 April. FashionCapital. https://www.fashioncapital.co.uk/insights/fashion-revolution-closes-uk-hq-but-the-movement-continues-with-2026-campaign/
- **Clean Up Australia Day 2027:** community clean-ups Sunday 7 March 2027, with organiser events also listed for 2 and 5 March (checked 15 Nov 2026). https://www.cleanup.org.au
- **NAIDOC Week 2027:** starts Sunday 4 July 2027 (preliminary). timeanddate. https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/australia/naidoc-week
- **World Cleanup Day:** 20 September, proclaimed by UN General Assembly resolution 78/122 (December 2023); UN-Habitat facilitates. https://www.un.org/en/observances/cleanup-day and https://www.awarenessdays.com/awareness-days-calendar/world-cleanup-day/
- **Earth Overshoot Day:** calculated each year by Global Footprint Network; 30 July in 2026. https://overshoot.footprintnetwork.org/2026-calculation/
- **GivingTuesday 2027:** Tuesday 30 November 2027, the Tuesday after US Thanksgiving (Thursday 25 November 2027). https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/world/giving-tuesday
- **World Kindness Day:** 13 November, World Kindness Movement. https://theworldkindnessmovement.org/home
- **World Environment Day 2027:** 5 June; host Serbia (Belgrade), theme Play for Humanity: Sport and Music for All (UNEP site, checked 15 Nov 2026). https://www.worldenvironmentday.global
- **Plastic Free July:** Plastic Free Foundation. https://www.plasticfreejuly.org
- **Month-long and organiser-run moments** (Veganuary, Dry January, Black History Month, Mental Health Awareness Month, Pride Month, Dry July, Breast Cancer Awareness Month, Movember): each organiser's own site, linked in the master list.

## Before you publish a post

Re-check the organiser's site. Themes, host countries and some dates are announced during the year, and organisations change.
