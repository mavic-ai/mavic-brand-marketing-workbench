## Why most social media holiday calendars don't work

Most lists give you a "holiday" for every day of the year, and most of those days were invented to fill a calendar. Posting on them makes a brand sound like every other brand posting on them.

The moments worth joining have a **social movement behind them**: an organiser, people who take part whether or not brands show up, and a clear ask. On those days a community is already paying attention and already sharing. A brand with a real part to play can add to that. A brand without one just adds noise.

So this toolkit does three things:

1. **A rhythm you can keep.** Feeds rank posts by what people engage with, and nobody engages with a brand that isn't there. Consistency you can sustain beats bursts you can't.
2. **One idea, every channel.** Each channel is a different room with different rules. Write the idea once, then a version for each room.
3. **A short list of moments that matter.** 45 moments for 2027 that passed four tests, and a fit test to pick the few that are yours.

## Step 1: Set your rhythm (15 minutes)

- **Channels.** Start with two or three you can keep up. You can add more later.
- **Posts a week.** Pick a number you could keep for 12 weeks without heroics. Starting points (rules of thumb): solo, 2 channels and 3 posts a week; small team, 3 channels and 5; marketing team, 4 or more channels and 10.
- **Themes.** Two to four recurring themes with weights that add up to 100%. Moments are anchors; themes fill the rest of the month.
- **Check-in.** Tick each week every planned post went out. If you miss two weeks running, lower the number rather than stopping.

## Step 2: One idea, every channel (15 minutes)

Write the core idea in one sentence, why your customer cares, and the proof (a number, a photo, a quote). Then write a version for each channel, within its limit and in its style. See [`channel-guide.md`](channel-guide.md).

Before you post each version, check:

- The first line works on its own. It may be all people see.
- It sounds like your brand, not like the platform.
- Links go where that channel lets people click.
- One call to action, not three.

## Step 3: Pick your moments (20 minutes)

Go through the master list and run the fit test on any moment that tempts you. Score each question 0 (no), 1 (somewhat) or 2 (yes):

1. **Our connection is genuine.** The cause touches what we make, who we serve or how we work.
2. **We can contribute, not just post.** We'll do something: switch off, give, clean up, change a practice.
3. **We have proof.** We can show it: a number, a photo, a partner.
4. **Its community would welcome us.** People who care about this cause would be glad we joined, not suspicious.

**7 or 8: Join. 5 or 6: Support quietly** (share the organiser's message, take part, give, without making it about you). **4 or less: Skip.** These thresholds are a rule of thumb.

Most brands should join four to ten moments a year (rule of thumb). Every entry has a **Join only if** line and a **Watch out** line. Read both before you plan.

## Step 4: Plan the year (10 minutes)

For each moment you're joining:

- Start planning at the lead time in the list (usually 3 to 8 weeks before).
- Use one of the three content angles, or your own, and write one idea per channel.
- Re-check the organiser's site: themes and some dates are announced during the year.

Then fill the rest of each month from your themes, at your weekly rhythm. The workbook builds the 2027 plan for you; `workbenches/content-plan/plan-2027.md` is the same plan as a file any AI tool can read.

## Do it in Mavic

In [Mavic](https://mavic.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=social_holidays_2027):

1. **Add the moments.** Paste the moments you chose into a chat and ask: "Add these holidays to my calendar". Public holidays for your country are already in the Calendar's Holidays panel.
2. **Ask for ideas.** For each moment, ask for post ideas for your channels, and type @ to tag a customer profile.
3. **Schedule.** Drag ideas onto dates in the Calendar and choose Create content. In the Post editor, turn on Customize per channel to edit each version, and use Rewrite > Shorten if one runs over.
4. **Approve.** Nothing posts until you approve it.

Help Centre: [Calendar](https://mavic.crisp.help/en/article/calendar-1k88zg/), [Getting ideas](https://mavic.crisp.help/en/article/getting-ideas-vfhpq4/), [Editing captions per platform](https://mavic.crisp.help/en/article/editing-captions-per-platform-1jihvy9/), [Content Theme](https://mavic.crisp.help/en/article/content-theme-do2x23/).

## Rules of thumb, not research

Cadence starting points, the fit test thresholds, "four to ten moments a year", lead times and the "what tends to work" notes in the channel guide are our starting points, not findings. Change them if your results say so. Character limits come from Mavic's Help Centre (checked 12 Nov 2026). Dates and organisers are in [`sources.md`](sources.md).

## Updating the list

The list is rebuilt each November for the following year. To suggest a moment, open an issue with the organiser's link and show how it passes the four tests in [`curation-rules.md`](curation-rules.md).
