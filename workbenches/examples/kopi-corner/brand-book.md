# Kopi Corner brand book

Kopi Corner is a fictional Singapore coffee shop, invented for teaching and for testing the skills in this repo. It is not a real business. Prices and details are made up.

How Kopi Corner sounds, on every channel. For anyone, or any AI tool, writing for us.

## Who we are

- **Archetype:** The Good Friend
- **Description:** We sound like the uncle behind the counter who remembers your order: warm, quick, a little cheeky, never fussy.

## Personality

### Warm (90)
- Sounds like: We sound like a person who's glad you're here.
- Does not mean: Gushing, sugary or over-familiar.
- In our voice: "Your usual? Kopi-C kosong, coming right up."

### Down-to-earth (70)
- Sounds like: Short, kind and plain, the way we talk across the counter.
- Does not mean: Sloppy or careless.
- In our voice: "Kaya toast is $1.80. Still the best deal on the street."

### Playful (50)
- Sounds like: We enjoy words and leave room for a smile.
- Does not mean: Silly, or jokey about serious things.
- In our voice: "Monday again? We added an extra shot. You are welcome."

## How we write, everywhere

### Contractions
- Do: Use them: we're, it's, you'll.
- Don't: Stiff phrasing: we are pleased to inform you.

### Be specific
- Do: Concrete details: the number, the place, the time. "Open from 6.30am, stall 15, by the market entrance."
- Don't: Vague praise: fast, reliable, quality.

### Talk to one person
- Do: Write to you: "You'll smell the toast before you see the stall."
- Don't: Talk about customers: "Customers love our toast."

### Prices and offers
- Do: The price and what's included, up front: "Kopi and two kaya toast, $4.20, till 10am."
- Don't: A from-price, with the catch in small print.

### Emoji
- Do: Only when they add meaning, 2 at most.
- Don't: An emoji after every line.

### Urgency
- Do: Real deadlines only: "Ends Sunday."
- Don't: Pressure that isn't true: "Only a few left!!!"

## Channel by channel

### Instagram
- Description: Quick, warm posts that make you want to drop by.
- Who it's for: Regulars and neighbours scrolling on the way to work, plus people looking for a breakfast spot nearby.
- Formality (1 to 5): 2
- Sentence length: Short: under 15 words
- How we open: A short story or moment
- How we close: A question that invites comments
- Formatting: Line breaks, 1 or 2 emoji
- Examples and data: Before and after: show, don't claim
- Product mentions: Named once, after the problem
- Example line: "6.30am. First batch of kaya toast is out. Who's here before us?"

### Facebook
- Description: News and offers for regulars, plain and trustworthy.
- Who it's for: Older regulars and families nearby who want opening hours, offers and news.
- Formality (1 to 5): 2
- Sentence length: Medium: 15 to 20 words
- How we open: Straight to the news
- How we close: One clear call to action
- Formatting: Short paragraphs, no emoji
- Examples and data: Customer stories over statistics
- Product mentions: Named once, after the problem
- Example line: "We're closed this Monday for a deep clean. Back Tuesday at 6.30am, kopi on as usual."

### WhatsApp and chat
- Description: Quick, friendly replies to orders and questions.
- Who it's for: Customers messaging to pre-order or ask if we're open.
- Formality (1 to 5): 1
- Sentence length: Very short: under 10 words
- How we open: A personal note
- How we close: A sign-off in our voice
- Formatting: Line breaks, 1 or 2 emoji
- Examples and data: Keep it light, no data
- Product mentions: Only when it answers the question
- Example line: "Yes, we're open! Your kopi will be ready in 5."

## Words we never use

- unleash -> start, let
- unlock -> open, start
- harness -> use
- delve -> look at
- elevate -> improve
- empower -> help, let
- game-changer -> say what changed
- revolutionary -> new, and say how
- cutting-edge -> new
- in today's fast-paced world -> cut it
- synergy -> say what works together
- leverage -> use
- dive deep -> look closely
- seamless -> say what's easy
- supercharge -> speed up
- mouth-watering -> describe the taste
- to die for -> describe the taste
- authentic -> say where the recipe comes from
- artisanal -> say who makes it and how
- delectable -> describe the taste
- best in town -> say what makes it different
- hidden gem -> say where we are
- foodie heaven -> say what's on the menu
- must-try -> say why they'd like it
- Instagrammable -> say what it looks like
- treat yourself -> say what they get

## Before anything goes out

- Read it out loud. Does it sound like the uncle behind the counter?
- Is it free of every word on the ban list?
- Is every price, time and address right today?
- Does it follow the channel's opening, closing and formatting?
