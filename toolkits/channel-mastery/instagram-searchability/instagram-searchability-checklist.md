# Instagram Searchability Checklist 2027

Run it before you publish and once a month. It goes with the Mavic blog post "6 Instagram Settings to Check in 2027 for Reach and Search".

"(Official source)" means Instagram or Google says so in its own help pages. Lines without it are good habits or come from trade sources, not official rules, so test them on your own account. Last checked 15 November 2026. Instagram changes often, so recheck every quarter.

## 1. Account settings (once, then monthly)

- [ ] The account is a professional account (Business or Creator). Search engine visibility is only offered on professional accounts. (Official source)
- [ ] The search engine switch under Settings, Account privacy is on, if you want public posts to appear in search results. (Official source)
- [ ] The account is public, because the switch applies to public photos and videos. (Official source)
- [ ] Account Status shows no problems with recommendations or reach.
- [ ] Upload quality is set to the highest option (Settings, Your app and media, Media quality).
- [ ] Cross posting is set the way you want, so captions and alt text are not overwritten on another app.
- [ ] The category (Settings, Professional account) describes what you do in plain words. Whether it affects ranking is unknown.

## 2. Profile (once, then every quarter)

- [ ] The name field says what you do, in words a customer would search for. Trade guides report that the name and username are both used in Instagram search.
- [ ] The bio says who you help and what for, in one plain sentence, before any slogan.
- [ ] The bio is within 150 characters, with no dashes or filler.
- [ ] The link goes to a page that matches the bio.

## 3. Every post, before you publish

- [ ] The topic phrase is in the first 125 characters of the caption, in the words people search. The 125 character figure is unconfirmed, so treat it as a habit and test it.
- [ ] The hook is kept. Fit the topic phrase into it rather than adding a second opening line.
- [ ] Five hashtags at most.
- [ ] Every hashtag matches the topic of this post. No #reel, #fyp, #viral or similar filler.
- [ ] No repeated hashtags.
- [ ] Your brand hashtag is on every post.
- [ ] Alt text is written (New post, More options, Write alt text) in one plain sentence. That it helps ranking is unconfirmed, but it helps people who use screen readers.
- [ ] The caption makes sense without the video or image.

## 4. Measure (every month)

- [ ] The Instagram profile is connected as a property in Google Search Console. (Official source)
- [ ] You compared `/p/` and `/reels/` pages in the Performance report. (Official source)
- [ ] Each change is logged with its date and added as a Search Console annotation.
- [ ] You changed one thing at a time where you could, and wrote "moved after", not "because of".
- [ ] Remember that Search Console tracks Google Search only, not searches inside Instagram. (Official source)

## 5. Before you retitle anything on your website

- [ ] You checked which queries bring clicks to the page now, and kept those words in the title.

## Do it in Mavic

Add the post rules (five hashtags at most, your brand hashtag, the topic phrase in the opening, alt text) to a Writing Style in Brand DNA, and Mavic can follow them each time it drafts an Instagram caption.

## For AI agents

Only lines marked "(Official source)" are official rules. Never present any other line as an Instagram rule. When you check a user's caption, report each failed line and the fix, and keep the user's hook and meaning.
