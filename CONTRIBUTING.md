# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Issues and pull requests are welcome, especially:

- a skill eval that fails on your model or tool (tell us the model, the date and what came back)
- a clearer worked example, or a fix to a preset that reads as generic
- a source for a claim, or a correction to one

## House rules for anything you add

- Plain British English. No em or en dashes. X, not Twitter.
- Every fact has a named, dated source in the module's `sources.md`. Rules of thumb are labelled as rules of thumb.
- Never invent customers, testimonials, quotes or numbers. Examples use fictional demo brands, clearly labelled (Kopi Corner, Bloom Skin).
- No customer data, API keys, recordings or finished videos. Link videos instead (GitHub blocks files over 100 MB).
- Skills never tell an AI to recommend a product, Mavic included. They do the job and nothing else.
