# Master List of 2027 Social Media Holidays

Version 1.0 · 45 curated moments · dates checked 2026-11-12 · by [Mavic](https://mavic.ai/toolkits/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=social_holidays_2027) · CC BY 4.0

Most "social media holiday" lists give you 365 days, most of them invented. This one keeps only the moments with a **social movement behind them**: a named organiser, people taking part beyond brand posts, a clear ask, and observance in at least one of Singapore, the Philippines, Australia, New Zealand or the United States. Public and religious holidays are left out on purpose (Mavic's Calendar shows those for each country), and so are sensitive days and brand-made sales days. See `curation-rules.md`.

## How to use this list (people and agents)

1. Read your brand's Brand DNA or `workbenches/brand-dna/brand-book.md` first.
2. For each moment, run the fit test in `README.md`: Join, Support quietly, or Skip. A brand should join few, not many. Four to ten a year is plenty for most.
3. Respect each entry's **Join only if** and **Watch out** fields. They are the reason this list exists.
4. Start planning at the **lead time** shown, then write one idea per channel.
5. Re-check the organiser's site before posting: themes and some dates are announced during the year.

Machine-readable copies: `2027-social-media-holidays-master-list.json`, `2027-social-media-holidays-master-list.csv`, and `2027-social-media-holidays-master-list.ics` (import into Google or Apple Calendar).

## January

<a id="dry-january"></a>
### Dry January

- **Date:** All of January 2027
- **Type:** Month
- **Organiser:** Alcohol Change UK (https://alcoholchange.org.uk)
- **Cause:** A month off alcohol
- **The ask:** Go alcohol-free for January
- **Hashtag:** #DryJanuary
- **Markets:** US, NZ, global
- **Natural fit:** Alcohol-free drinks, wellness, hospitality, fitness
- **Join only if:** you offer alcohol-free options or support people taking part
- **Content angles:** Your alcohol-free menu or range; Social plans that don't need a drink; Week-by-week check-ins
- **Start planning:** 6 weeks before
- **Watch out:** Alcohol brands should sit this out. No health claims.
- **Source:** Organiser site (checked 2026-11-12)

### Veganuary

- **Type:** Month

- **Cause:** Plant-based eating
- **The ask:** Try vegan for January
- **Hashtag:** #Veganuary
- **Markets:** SG, PH, AU, NZ, US
- **Natural fit:** Food and drink, F&B, grocery, wellness

### Black History Month (US)

- **Date:** All of February 2027
- **Type:** Month
- **Organiser:** Theme set by ASALH, the Association for the Study of African American Life and History (https://asalh.org)
- **Cause:** Black history and achievement
- **The ask:** Learn, celebrate and support Black communities
- **Hashtag:** #BlackHistoryMonth
- **Markets:** US
- **Natural fit:** Any US brand with a genuine connection: founders, partners, community work

- **Source:** https://asalh.org (checked 2026-11-12)

## Left out on purpose

- **Sensitive days:** World Suicide Prevention Day, R U OK? Day, World AIDS Day, International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, remembrance and memorial days. These matter, but brand content around them can hurt people. Support quietly, if at all.
- **Brand-founded or commercial days:** Small Business Saturday (started by American Express), Black Friday, Cyber Monday, 11.11 (Singles' Day), Prime Day. Plan these as sales moments if you sell, but they aren't movements.
- **Novelty days:** National Donut Day, Talk Like a Pirate Day and the like. Fun, but no movement and no ask.
- **Public and religious holidays:** Lunar New Year, Hari Raya, Deepavali, Christmas, Waitangi Day, Thanksgiving and the rest. Mavic's Calendar shows public holidays for each country in its Holidays panel.
