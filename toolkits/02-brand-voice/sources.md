# Sources: brand voice

## Research

- **Long inputs: information in the middle is used less well than at the start and end.**
  Liu, N. F., Lin, K., Hewitt, J., Paranjape, A., Bevilacqua, M., Petroni, F. and Liang, P. (2024). Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts. *Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics*, 12, 157 to 173. https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/
  Used for: "Give it too much, and the rules that matter get buried."

## Rules of thumb (not research)

These come from practice, and are labelled as such wherever they appear. Change them if your own tests disagree.

- 3 to 5 personality traits
- 5 to 8 do and don't rules
- 20 to 30 ban list entries
- Five test posts, read aloud
- The channel defaults in `presets.md`
